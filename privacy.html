Privacy Policy

Health Feed, a personal health-data dashboard app by Robert Gamble

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Who this applies to

Health Feed is a private, single-user app. Robert Gamble built it and is its only user. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data the app gets

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's OAuth sign-in, the app reads the owner's own health and fitness data (activity, sleep, and health measurements) from Google Health / Fitbit through Google APIs. It only asks for read-only access.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show the owner a personal dashboard of his own data. It is stored only on the owner's private computer. It is never uploaded to this website or to any third-party server.

Sharing

The data is never sold, rented, shared, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, it is not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and it is not used to judge creditworthiness or for lending.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Health Feed's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete stored data from his own computer at any time. Deleting the local files removes all copies.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Choose the app, then choose "Remove access". Once access is removed, the app can no longer read any data.
