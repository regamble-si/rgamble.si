Sorting Station

An Android app that turns an overhead phone over a mat into a basement sorting station.

How it works

  1. Place an item on the mat under the phone.
  2. The app captures it automatically, or you press a foot pedal or just say “go”.
  3. It identifies the item by barcode or with a vision model.
  4. It assigns the item a bin.
  5. It speaks the result out loud, so your hands stay free.

Status

Built in small slices. Currently on slice 1.5 (hands-free triggers: auto-capture, pedal, and offline voice commands). Work in progress.
